01 / Profile

Good decisions ride on data a team can trust. That trust gets built at the schema, the pipeline, and the validation step, long before it reaches a slide.

Two-plus years of production work go into data warehouses, KPI dashboards, ETL pipelines, and self-service tooling that non-technical teams actually use. A single day might run from a normalized PostgreSQL schema and a discrepancy investigation to a churn or pricing recommendation in front of leadership.

The research side runs deeper than most analyst roles ask for. Eleven papers published across five conferences in 2026, three as first author, two as corresponding author in Procedia Computer Science, and two winners of an ABR Best Paper Award, span agent architectures over fine-tuned LLMs, AI-assisted audit and assurance, and human-AI collaboration, alongside review work for international IS conferences. An M.S. in Business Analytics (GPA 3.8) and two national first-place finishes, an AI hackathon and an IT security competition, sit behind the résumé.
